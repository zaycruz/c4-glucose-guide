Challenge 4 · Meals, timing and movement

A short walk after dinner, and an earlier dinner

From 12 weeks of glucose sensor data, it measures how meals, meal timing and movement shape each survivor's blood sugar, and turns that into advice they can follow. Every number in the advice traces to its evidence.

What it means for a care team

A walk after dinner is the simplest advice with the largest measured effect, and 10 minutes gets most of it. mg/dL is the unit on a glucose meter.

What to do

Suggest a short walk after dinner and an earlier dinner. Survivors with frequent lows get a low-sugar caution and are never told to fast longer.

For each survivor

A forecast of their own dinner rise

Gradient boosting forecasts the rise after a survivor's typical dinner, each meal by a model that never saw it. Typical miss 14 mg/dL. Personal effects are shown only where the person's own data supports them; otherwise the advice says so and uses the cohort number.

Know the limits

  • Walking was self-chosen, so the walk effect is an association. It passes a negative control.
  • In real people the effects point the same way but are much smaller and not proven yet.
  • Night-shift workers get no clock-time advice.

Endpoints

JSON over HTTPS, no key needed. Contract in CONTRACT.md.

  • GET/api/c4/survivors→The roster: 50 survivors with status, a plain headline and up to four signal tags.
  • GET/api/c4/survivors/:id→One survivor: recommendations, each number tied to evidence, safety flags and a patient view in English, Spanish and Kreyòl.
  • GET/api/c4/survivors/:id/advice→Warm, plain-language advice. Precomputed; rewrites with untraced numbers are rejected.
  • GET/api/c4/cohort→Program-wide effects with CIs, the randomized trial, the real-data check and a plain-language summary.
  • GET/api/c4/verification→The published verification record that the end-to-end check compares with the live data.

Reproduce

Raw data stays out of the repo; only the derived JSON is committed.

github.com/zaycruz/c4-glucose-guide →
  1. npm install

    The machine-learning step runs on a Proxmox node first: scripts/build-ml.sh (README › Run).

  2. scripts/build-data.sh <data dir>

    Builds the data the Worker serves from the Challenge 4 download. About 10 seconds.

  3. node scripts/e2e.mjs

    Checks every endpoint, and that every number in every recommendation is in its cited evidence.
